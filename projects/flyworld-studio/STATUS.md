- Shared native XYZ is preserved with uniform unit conversion to µm. Coarse source skeleton cables and synapse positions are not warped or individually centered. Reference surfaces use a labeled 2-µm preview grid. Anatomical axis directions, current mutation alignment and avatar registration remain unverified.
- Five tested strongest downstream pairs have matching aggregate and individual contact counts: **86, 95, 149, 177 and 5** at confidence ≥0.5. This is bounded validation, not proof of every source pair or biological function.
- **199 application tests, 20 preprocessing tests, 18 anatomy tests and production build pass.** The browser suite contains 11 scenarios. Prior nine-scenario user-run acceptance predates the atlas and takeoff/courtship follow-up; those additions still need browser/human verification.
